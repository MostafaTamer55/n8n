Our Heritage

At Abo Saad, we believe food is a celebration of culture. Every dish we serve tells a story of the Arabian Peninsula—from the smoky fires of desert camps to the bustling spice markets. We source authentic ingredients to bring you the rich, warm flavors that define Saudi hospitality.

Our Specialties

The Royal Menu

كبسة

Traditional Kabsa

SAR 45

The national dish. Fragrant basmati rice cooked in a rich broth with special spices, topped with tender roasted chicken, almonds, and sweet raisins.

مندي

Meat Mandi

SAR 65

Premium local lamb, slow-cooked in an underground tandoor. Served over smoky, saffron-infused rice that melts in your mouth.

جريش

Najdi Jareesh

SAR 35

A comforting, savory porridge made from crushed wheat, slowly simmered with yogurt, meat, and topped with caramelized onions and ghee.

سليق

Taif Saleeg

SAR 40

A creamy, risotto-like dish from the Hejaz region. Short-grain rice cooked with milk and meat broth, served with roasted chicken and spicy Dakkous sauce.

مطبق

Mutabbaq

SAR 20

A beloved street food classic. Folded pan-fried pastry stuffed with savory minced meat, beaten eggs, leeks, and a blend of aromatic spices.

حنيذ

Asiri Haneeth

SAR 70

A southern specialty. Meat slow-roasted in a traditional Markh plant-lined oven, giving it an unmistakable smoky flavor and fall-off-the-bone tenderness.
